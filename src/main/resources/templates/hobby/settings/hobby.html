Hobby Publication and Closure
Click the button when you are ready to make your hobby public to other users.
Make sure you've registered the introduction, banner image, hobby topics, and activity areas.
When you publish the hobby, notifications will be sent to users interested in the main activity areas and hobby topics.
Close the hobby if you have finished your hobby activities.
Once the hobby is closed, you can no longer recruit members or create meetings, and you cannot modify the hobby path and name.
Records of hobby meetings and participating members will be preserved.

Remove Hobby
Deleting a hobby will remove all records related to the hobby and cannot be recovered.
Hobbies that meet the following criteria will be automatically deleted:
  • Private hobbies that are more than 1 week old
  • Hobbies that have not created any meetings for a month after being published
  • Hobbies that were closed without creating any meetings after publication
